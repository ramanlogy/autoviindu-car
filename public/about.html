Legal & Compliance

Terms of Service & Privacy

Our clear, simple policies designed to protect our buyers, sellers, and platform integrity.

1. Terms and Conditions of Use

Welcome to AutoViindu. By accessing our website, using our comparison tool, EMI calculators, or listing cars for sale, you agree to comply with the following terms:

  • Listing Authenticity: Sellers must guarantee that vehicle details, manufactured years, and number of owners submitted via the "Sell Your Car" form are accurate and match official DOTM Bluebook records.
  • Manually Verified listings: All listings undergo a manual 24-hour review before publication. AutoViindu reserves the right to reject listings that contain misleading information.
  • No Commercial Abuse: The platform is designed for private and authorized sellers. Scraping database details, image assets, or technical data is strictly prohibited.
2. Privacy & Data Protection Policy

We respect your privacy. All user registration inputs, telephone numbers, and email addresses submitted through our service booking forms are protected under the following guidelines:

  • Buyer Privacy: We only share a seller's phone number with verified, genuinely interested buyers to prevent spam.
  • No Third-Party Sharing: Your personal data is never sold or rented to third-party advertising companies.
  • Information Security: Financial calculator parameters and uploaded vehicle photos are stored securely using standard encryption protocols.
3. Listing Guidelines & Fees

Listing your vehicle on AutoViindu is completely free. To maintain listing quality, please ensure:

  • Upload up to 8 high-resolution photos showing clear exterior, interior, and odometer views.
  • Provide accurate details on number of owners, engine/fuel specs, and asking price in NPR.
  • Notify us or update your status once the vehicle is sold to remove it from our active database.
